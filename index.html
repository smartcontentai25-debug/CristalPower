Profile & Biography

Elevating energy across global dance floors.

Hailing from Caracas, Venezuela, and based in Miami, FL, CRISTAL POWER has quickly established herself as one of the most compelling emerging figures in the Tech House, Deep House, and Afro House scenes. With over 6 years of pristine experience behind the decks, her signature sound blends infectious grooves, deep basslines, and an electrifying vibe inspired by iconic clubs and underground culture.

She has brought her sonic vision to legendary stages such as the iconic Burning Man festival (Black Rock Desert - 2025 & 2026), Miami's nightlife epicenter Factory Town, and featured on world-renowned broadcasting platforms like Ibiza Global Radio.

Her discography continues to gain steady support across international platforms and club circuits through labels like Kubbo Records, Valera Under Records, Drogalipz Jamzz, and XIMA, solidifying a fresh, powerful, and immersive artistic identity.

Technical & Hospitality Riders.

Mandatory specifications for bookings and event production.

Technical Rider

Audio Spec
  • Media Players: 2x or 3x Pioneer CDJ-3000 (Updated firmware connected via Ethernet DJ Link).
  • Mixer: 1x Pioneer DJM-900 NXS2 (In mint working condition).
  • Effects: Pioneer RMX-1000 Station + official stand (if applicable).
  • Monitors: 2x High-power booth monitors adjustable to artist ear level.
  • Tech Support: Qualified audio technician present during soundcheck and performance.

Hospitality Rider

Logistics
  • Booth Catering: 1x Bottle Santa Teresa Rum, 2x Coca-Cola, 2x Red Bull Sugarfree.
  • Hydration & Amenities: 3x Cold still water bottles, ice bucket with clean ice, cups, and black booth towels.
  • Guest List: 5x VIP / Backstage Access Passes for guests and crew.
  • Accommodation: 4-Star or 3-Star Superior hotel near the venue (King/Single room).
